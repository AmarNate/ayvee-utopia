Her. Water seen from just above, and it's fun. Never still, never an edge, never scary.
The breath. The whole surface lifts on the voice and drops on the exhale. A held note raises her. Silence lets her settle.
The wave. Bass sends one wave in from the side, and when it breaks it throws foam - a burst of bubbles that rise and pop.
The fizz. Highs are bubbles - they surface on the crests, rise a little, pop. The busier the top end, the fizzier she is.
The stir. Synths stir her: the ripples run faster, the colour churns, and the whole surface swirls around a slow eddy.
Her. One spark, loose in a dark room, never still. A hard point with almost no halo. Faint is not gentle.
The trail. Where she's been cools behind her, white to red to nothing. Longer the louder it gets.
The crack. Bass breaks her apart: shards fly, fall, cool. She goes dark for a beat and re-lights.
The heat. The voice makes her burn hotter - whiter, denser, never bigger. Synths make her restless. Highs make her flicker.
The coals. Dormant embers on the floor wake for a moment when she passes close, then go back to sleep.
The figure. Nine hundred points swirling in three dimensions inside one body, ten colours that drift with the highs. A twin vortex kicks in with the synths, spiral arms twist with the intensity, and some points leave ribbon trails that grow with the volume.
The edge. The silhouette plus two faint rings are the physical boundary, seen in the round. It breathes with the voice, it never breaks.
The grit. White-hot sparks where the body presses against its own edge. Trenje.
The lights. Drifting patches that flicker on their own, faster with the highs. At least one is always lit.
The bursts. Pops of colour on bass hits, or wherever you tap.
The meter. Four bars, top right: bass, synths, voice, highs, each read against its own floor - a quiet layer still counts once it rises above its floor, and Sensitivity is how far above the floor it has to rise. Bass kicks the edge and its pulse sets the swirl speed (eighths swirl faster than quarters), synths rock the twin vortex, voice swells the body, highs flicker the lights.
