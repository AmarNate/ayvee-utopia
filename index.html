Ay-Vee Utopia

A place for people who enjoy sound as a picture. Seven embodiments live here and four are awake: Bod-e, who holds a shape and bursts against it anyway; Gali, who is water and thinks that's hilarious; Hingez, a frame that frequency barely keeps shut; Wren, one spark that will not sit still.

And Pelvy rides with all of them. He gathers the Echoes you earn by staying, and when he is full he gives them back as a Focused Frequency.

You and Pelvy.
Pelvy. He emits nothing of his own. He gives back what the embodiment just did, a beat late, lower and wider, near the floor, and the deepest bass (the part you feel more than hear) pushes him lower. The only direction he knows is down.
His stacks. Every hundred Echoes is one stack. He holds three at most; past that he trembles, full and wanting to give it away. Focus is you letting him: he turns what he gathered into the Focused Frequency and catches it again on the way down.
Him. A slab of something old and wrong: dark glass with veins of dull metal, cracked and fused back together. It never shines. Light only scrapes its edges.
The hinges. No pins. Every hinge is a string of frequency stretched across a seam, and it is the only thing holding the pieces together. The low ones listen to the bass, the middle ones to the voice and the synths, the top ones to the highs.
Unhinging. When a hinge's sound hits, its string shakes wider and the slab swings open a few degrees; light and motes leak out of the seam. Too loud and the string loses its tune for a moment and the slab hangs open, then it pulls taut and snaps back with a flash along the seam.
The inside. On the chorus every string buzzes, the frame hangs half open and whatever is inside pours out through the seams. In the quiet he sits there closed, and far too calm.
Tap a seam to knock that hinge open.
Her. Water seen from just above, and it's fun. Never still, never an edge, never scary.
The breath. The whole surface lifts on the voice and drops on the exhale. A held note raises her. Silence lets her settle.
The wave. Bass sends one wave in from the side, and when it breaks it throws foam - a burst of bubbles that rise and pop.
The fizz. Highs are bubbles - they surface on the crests, rise a little, pop. The busier the top end, the fizzier she is.
The stir. Synths stir her: the ripples run faster, the colour churns, and the whole surface swirls around a slow eddy.
Her. One spark, loose in a dark room, never still. A hard point with almost no halo. Faint is not gentle.
The trail. Where she's been cools behind her, white to red to nothing. Longer the louder it gets.
The crack. Bass breaks her apart: shards fly, fall, cool. She goes dark for a beat and re-lights.
The heat. The voice makes her burn hotter - whiter, denser, never bigger. Synths make her restless. Highs make her flicker.
The coals. Dormant embers on the floor wake for a moment when she passes close, then go back to sleep.
The figure. Nine hundred points swirling in three dimensions inside one body, each one a curved ribbon that tapers behind it, ten colours that drift with the highs. A twin vortex kicks in with the synths, spiral arms twist with the intensity, and the ribbons grow with the volume.
The edge. The silhouette plus two faint rings are the physical boundary, seen in the round. It breathes with the voice, it never breaks.
The grit. White-hot sparks where the body presses against its own edge. Trenje.
The lights. Drifting patches that flicker on their own, faster with the highs. At least one is always lit.
The bursts. Pops of colour on bass hits, or wherever you tap.
The meter. Four bars, top right: bass, synths, voice, highs, each read against its own floor - a quiet layer still counts once it rises above its floor, and Sensitivity is how far above the floor it has to rise. Bass kicks the edge and its pulse sets the swirl speed (eighths swirl faster than quarters), synths rock the twin vortex, voice swells the body, highs flicker the lights. The Lab (on a computer, bottom right; on a phone, in More) shows it in numbers and Record writes it to a file.
