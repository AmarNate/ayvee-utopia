## 3 · What was done

**The light, per second (Bod-e).** Everything she draws now goes through one number, LK: how much of a frame's light this frame adds, so that a second adds up to the same on any machine. The reference is the picture at 20 frames a second, as ruled.
- Focus opening, middle of the frame, plain white: 40 to 50% before, **0.00 to 0.01% after**.
- Same picture on a slow and a fast pace: Telo's chorus reads 13.9 at 25 frames a second and 14.6 at a 60 pace (average brightness of the frame, 0 to 255).

**Auto follows the song's own arc.** It holds how loud the song is now against the loudest it has been. At its own top the song is in its chorus; a tenth below, in its verse.
- Growing Pain at 2:05: intensity 1.39 before, **0.56 after**. Its chorus at 1:40: 1.60.
- Run offline on all eight songs: the dips land where the quiet parts are (Growing Pain 1:45 to 2:15, Possession around 1:20, Your Type around 3:15, Astro Radio Disco's intro and its last 25 seconds). Telo, right now and lowerr stay in chorus nearly the whole way, because they are loud nearly the whole way.
- Known limit, as on the chart: it only knows the song so far, so verses before the first big chorus read high.

**Sensitivity sits by Auto.** On the computer it is a well in the bottom bar, right after Auto. On the phone it is the row under Auto in More. It is gone from the Songs pane.

**Wall lights (Bod-e).** Small lamps with a core. Each keeps to one end of the window's longer side and slides along that wall. On a 1920 wide window the nearest a lamp's glow comes to the middle is 476 px on an ordinary day; she reaches 426 px at her widest and farthest drift.

**Bod-e, everyday.** She fights her own walls: on a beat she strikes a spot on her outline, the wall gives there, the points near it lunge, a tick of light and a magenta arc leave the spot. Her points crowd her wall. Two faint magenta rings hang around her. Her middle light has a core.

**Bod-e, Focus.** No white bloom: the bloom is her magenta, it stays near her and is gone once she is the room, so the middle stays dark with her light in it. The rings are the everyday rings turned up: 3 for one stack, 4 for two, 5 for three, pulsing with the bass from two stacks up.

**Gali.** Her three lights are small far lamps with cores (they were glows nearly half the window wide). Each lays a flickering path of light down her water. Her troughs fall into shadow so the swell reads as depth. The sea-flowers lie flat on her water, each with its own small light and ripple.

**Wren.** His body is a real cinder: a crust in nine facets, fire in the cracks, the hottest point low, turning slowly, heat strings off his back. One cinder in three is thrown to land, and stays on the floor as an ember cooling gold, orange, red. His light on the floor is 2.2 times wider and gentler. The floor lines inside it turn gold. A crack drops a ring on the floor.

**Clip.** 28 million bits a second (it was 8). A faint grain over the clip only. MP4 with AAC sound is asked for by its full name first. The bubble says what was made: "Clip saved: MP4, AAC sound." or "MP4, Opus sound (some players play it mute)." or "WebM."

## 4 · Beyond the ask (each easy to strip)

- **A quiet song.** A song that never comes near where Auto's top starts (a voice and a guitar) would have sat in Verse for a minute. After 12 seconds without reaching its top, the top comes down to the song. None of your eight songs trigger it: their arcs are identical with and without it.
- **Mic and Tab** start Auto's top from what they hear in their first second, since they have no usual level.
- **Wren's last place is cleared each frame.** A solid body smeared into a tube of its own copies under the slow wipe.
- **Words that had to change** (word geometry, Article 21): the tour's Songs step lost "Sensitivity decides how quiet a layer can be and still count."; the mood step gained "Sensitivity, beside it, decides how quiet a layer can be and still count."; the phone's More step lists Sensitivity; Auto's hover line reads "Auto follows the song's own arc."; the Clip bubble as above.
- **A narrow computer window** (under 1760 px wide): the bottom row has no room for one more well, so Sensitivity sits just above the bar, over Auto.
- **Auto's own clock.** The arc counts real seconds itself, so a busy machine cannot slow it down.
